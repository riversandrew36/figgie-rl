# Why the old Experts trailed Intermediate

Players market, 60 seconds, development seeds 1–400. Each subject played the
unchanged Hunter–Banker–Detective trio. Bluffer was the best Intermediate.
Cells show the mean and a paired 95% interval for the difference versus Bluffer.
Profit equals trading cash plus goal-card and majority payouts, less the 50-chip
ante. Activity shares and markouts are averaged per deal, with zero for a deal
that has no applicable trades. Markout values a goal card at 10 and omits majority.

The Experts traded more, sold for cash, and ended with fewer goal cards and
roughly half Bluffer’s majority payout. Their buy markouts were generally less
negative than Bluffer’s, so the data do not support overpaying as the main reason.
Inventory skew and frequent liquidation are consistent with the majority shortfall.
These observations do not isolate the six-card cliff or tape weighting.
